Everything you need to manage your inventory

SmartStock brings all your inventory operations together in one powerful, easy-to-use platform.

Inventory Tracking

Track real-time stock levels across multiple warehouses. Know exactly what you have, where it is, and when you're running low — all in one dashboard.

Smart Alerts

Automatic low stock and out-of-stock notifications keep you informed before problems arise. Set custom thresholds and never miss a reorder point again.

Supplier Management

Manage supplier relationships and purchase orders efficiently. Keep track of vendor contacts, lead times, and order history in one organized place.

Warehouse Management

Monitor multiple warehouse locations, capacities, and utilization. Optimize your storage layout and transfer stock between locations seamlessly.

Analytics & Reports

Detailed reports and visual analytics help you make smarter decisions. Identify trends, forecast demand, and understand your inventory performance at a glance.

Product Movement

Track every stock-in, stock-out, and transfer transaction with full audit logs. Maintain complete traceability and accountability for every inventory change.
